# Adding Stabilizing Legs to Top Plate

Adding Stabilizing Legs to Top Plate

We are adding four new stabilizing legs to the TopPlate to enhance the overall stability of the drone structure. These new legs will be identical in dimension to the existing Landing Leg 1 (Leg1), measuring eighteen millimeters by eighteen millimeters by seventy-four millimeters. We will use Part primitives and the Part::Boolean operation to attach these new legs to the underside of the TopPlate.
