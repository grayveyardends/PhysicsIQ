# Step 1 — Add Internal Frames to BottomPlate and TopPlate

Create two new Part objects: 'BottomPlate_Frame' (Part primitive: Box, dimensions 100x100x3mm) and 'TopPlate_Frame' (Part primitive: Box, dimensions 100x100x3mm). Position the 'BottomPlate_Frame' centrally on the 'Bottom Plate (PDB)' object, ensuring it is fully contained within its bounding box and does not overlap other components. Position the 'TopPlate_Frame' similarly centrally on the 'Top Plate' object. These frames will act as stiffeners to increase the rigidity of the plates. Use the Part → Box command, set the dimensions explicitly, and place them using the absolute coordinates (assuming the plate origin is at (0,0,0)): BottomPlate_Frame at (40,38,0) to (140,88,3), TopPlate_Frame at (40,38,0) to (140,88,3).
