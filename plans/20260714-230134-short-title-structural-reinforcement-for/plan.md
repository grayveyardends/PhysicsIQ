# **Short Title:** Structural Reinforcement for Drone Stability

**Short Title:** Structural Reinforcement for Drone Stability

**What we are building:** We are reinforcing the existing drone structure to improve its security against impacts and vibrations, and its overall stability during operation. This involves adding internal frames to the plates, upgrading the standoffs to larger, stronger versions, reinforcing the motor mounts and landing legs, and finally performing a stress analysis to validate the changes. We assume the material properties are standard for the given dimensions and that the modifications will not interfere with existing clearances or functions.

4. **Reinforce LandingLegs with Internal Stringers**  → `step-4.md`
5. **Run Stress Analysis on Modified Drone Structure**  → `step-5.md`
